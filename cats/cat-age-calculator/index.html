How the cat age calculator works

Up to 2 years it reads International Cat Care's chart, which goes month by month at first: about 4 human years at 3 months, 10 at 6 months, 12 at 7 months, 15 at a year, 21 at 18 months and 24 at 2 years. Between two rows we read the chart on a straight line, so a cat of 9 months comes out at about 13. That step is our arithmetic, not the charity's.

From 2 years the rule takes over: start at 24 and add 4 for each year past the second birthday. A 9-year-old cat is 24 + 7 × 4 = 52. We spread each year's 4 over its months, so a cat of 2 years and 6 months is about 26.

Cats Protection's chart uses the same steps: 15 at 12 months, 21 at 18 months, 24 at 2 years, then around four human years for each cat year. The one place the two differ is 14, which Cats Protection puts at 70 and International Cat Care at 72. The calculator follows International Cat Care.

Cat life stages in the calculator

The life stage is International Cat Care's:

  1. Under 7 months.
  2. Junior, 7 months to 2 years: full size, like a teenager or young adult in people.
  3. Adult, 3 to 6 years. Cats Protection calls this stage prime.
  4. Mature, 7 to 10 years, which both charities liken to a person in their mid-40s to mid-50s.
  5. Senior, 11 to 14 years.
  6. Super senior, 15 years and over.

VCA Animal Hospitals draws the senior line earlier, at about 9 to 11 for cats of all sizes. The stages are a guide to what to expect, and your vet may see your own cat differently.

Why there is no size or breed box

Because the sources have none. International Cat Care and Cats Protection each give one chart for all cats, and VCA gives one senior line for cats of every size. Dogs are different: their charts split by weight, and our dog age calculator asks for size.

What the number is good for

Picturing where your cat is in its life. A 12-year-old cat at about 64 is in the senior years, even if it still takes the stairs two at a time. The figure says nothing about health, so take questions about that to your vet.

Questions people ask

How does a cat age calculator work?

This one uses International Cat Care's chart. Up to 2 years it reads the chart's rows by month, on a straight line between two rows; from 2 years it starts at 24 and adds 4 human years for each cat year. It also names the life stage. Every figure is an estimate.

Is a cat year 7 human years?

No. Both International Cat Care and Cats Protection count the first two years as about 24 human years, because a cat grows up fast, and about 4 human years for each year after that. Seven a year would make a fully grown 2-year-old cat 14.

How old is a 15 year old cat in human years?

About 76, in both the International Cat Care and the Cats Protection charts. Both charities call cats of 15 and over super senior.

When is a cat a senior?

International Cat Care and Cats Protection put the senior stage at 11 to 14 years, with super senior from 15. VCA Animal Hospitals puts senior at about 9 to 11 for cats of all sizes. Your vet will judge your own cat.
