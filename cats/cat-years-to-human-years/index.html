In short

  • Rule of thumb: a cat's first two years equal about 24 human years, and each year after that about 4.
  • So a 5-year-old cat is about 36, a 10-year-old about 56, a 15-year-old about 76 and a 21-year-old about 100.
  • The chart comes from cat charities (International Cat Care, Cats Protection); vets' own life stages give no human ages.
  • Vets group cats as young adult (1 to 6), mature adult (7 to 10) and senior (10 and older). The ages are a guide, not a rule.
  • UK cats live about 11.7 years on average, and many reach their late teens.

To turn cat years into human years, count a cat's first two years as about 24 human years, then add 4 for each year after that. A 5-year-old cat is about 36, a 10-year-old about 56 and a 15-year-old about 76. That is the rule International Cat Care uses, and Cats Protection's chart gives the same numbers.

How to convert cat years to human years

Use the two-step rule:

  1. If your cat is 2 or older, start with 24.
  2. Add 4 for every year past the second birthday.
  3. For a cat under 2, read the age off the chart: 15 at one year, 21 at 18 months.

So a 9-year-old cat is 24 + (7 × 4) = 52. A 17-year-old is 24 + (15 × 4) = 84. Our cat age calculator does the sum for you, months included.

The numbers are a way to picture where a cat is in its life, not a medical measurement. International Cat Care says the comparison helps people understand a cat's life stage. Your vet will look at your cat, not at a chart.

Why the first two years count for so much

Because cats grow up fast. In International Cat Care's description, by the junior stage (7 months to 2 years) a cat has reached full size, a stage it compares to teenagers and young adults in people. Cats Protection says that after three years a cat has developed as much as a 28-year-old human.

That is also why the popular rule of seven human years per cat year does not work. Seven would make a 2-year-old cat a 14-year-old human, when the cat is already fully grown. After the fast start, both charities count about 4 human years per cat year.

The 2021 AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage Guidelines, written by a task force for vets, give no human-age equivalents. They also say any age grouping is an arbitrary line along a spectrum, not an absolute, and that some cats of 10 and over stay in such good shape that their vet may treat them as mature adults.

So a 7-year-old cat is in the same "mature" stage in all three systems, and the human-years chart puts it at 44.

How old is my cat in human years? Common ages

The ages people ask about most, from the chart:

  • 3 years: 28
  • 5 years: 36
  • 7 years: 44
  • 10 years: 56
  • 15 years: 76
  • 17 years: 84
  • 20 years: 96

Cats Protection likens the whole mature stage, 7 to 10, to a person in their mid-40s to mid-50s.

For a dog, the sums are different; see our page on dog years to human years.

How long do cats live?

International Cat Care says cats live on average for around 12 to 14 years, and that it is not unusual for some to reach their late teens or early twenties. Cats Protection gives 11.7 years as the average for pet cats in the UK.

That figure matches a 2024 study of UK vet records from the VetCompass programme, built from 7,936 cat deaths in 2019. It found a life expectancy at birth of 11.74 years. Female cats could expect 12.51 years and males 11.18. Of the 12 breeds analysed, counting crossbreds as one, Burmese and Birman had the longest life expectancy, about 14.4 years.

The record is far beyond any of these. Guinness World Records lists Creme Puff, of Austin, Texas, born on 3 August 1967, who lived 38 years and 3 days.

Keeping a note of your cat at this age

The chart says where your cat is. It does not say what your cat is like at 4, or 11, or 16: which chair, which window, which time of day. Those details change slowly and are easy to lose.

Questions people ask

How do you convert cat years to human years?

Count the first two years as 24 human years, then add 4 human years for every cat year after that. A 6-year-old cat is 24 + 16 = 40. This is the rule International Cat Care gives, and the Cats Protection chart follows it. It is an estimate for comparing life stages, not a medical measure.

How old is a 10 year old cat in human years?

About 56. Two years count as 24, and the eight years after that add 32. That is International Cat Care's figure, and Cats Protection's rule of about four human years a year after age three gives the same. Cats Protection describes 7 to 10 as the 'mature' stage, like a person in their mid-40s to mid-50s.

How old is a 15 year old cat in human years?

About 76, according to both the International Cat Care and Cats Protection charts. International Cat Care calls cats of 15 and over 'super senior'. Many cats reach that age, and the oldest cat on record, Creme Puff from Texas, lived to 38 years and 3 days.

Is a cat year 7 human years?

No. Cat charity charts count the first two years as about 24 human years, because a cat grows up fast, then about 4 human years for each cat year after that. Seven per year would make a 2-year-old cat 14, when it is already fully grown.

How long do cats live?

Around 12 to 14 years on average, International Cat Care says, and late teens or early twenties are not unusual. A 2024 study of UK vet records found a life expectancy at birth of 11.74 years, with females living about 1.3 years longer than males.
