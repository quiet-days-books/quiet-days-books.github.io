In short

  • In a 2024 study of UK dogs, standard Dachshunds had a median lifespan of 13.2 years and miniature Dachshunds 14.0.
  • Only 11 of 155 breeds in that study had a longer median than the miniature Dachshund.
  • Weight cost Dachshunds more than most breeds: normal-weight Dachshunds lived a median 16.4 years and overweight ones 14.1 in a 2019 study.
  • Back trouble is common: 27% of UK Dachshunds aged 10 or older had owner-reported signs of disc disease, most often in smooth-haired dogs.
  • By the AAHA 25% rule, a standard Dachshund is a senior from about 9.9 and a miniature from about 10.5.

Dachshunds usually live about 13 to 14 years. In the largest study so far, of UK dogs, standard Dachshunds had a median lifespan of 13.2 years and miniature Dachshunds 14.0, both above the 12.5 average for all dogs. The American Kennel Club lists 12 to 16 years, and one verified miniature Dachshund reached 21.

How long do dachshunds live on average?

The best measured figures come from a 2024 study by the charity Dogs Trust, published in Scientific Reports. It pooled records on 584,734 UK dogs from 18 sources, including insurers, vet groups, breed registries and rehoming charities, and it lists standard and miniature Dachshunds separately:

Standard Dachshund 2,305 926 13.2 years
Miniature Dachshund 7,342 1,655 14.0 years

Both groups include long-haired, smooth-haired and wire-haired dogs; the miniature group also includes the rabbit (Kaninchen) varieties. A median is the age by which half the dogs have died, so plenty of Dachshunds live well past it.

The large UK life tables published by the Royal Veterinary College in 2022, built from VetCompass vet records, covered 18 common breeds, and the Dachshund was not one of them.

Do miniature dachshunds live longer than standard?

In this data, yes, by about 0.8 years. The study's 95% confidence ranges were 13.0 to 13.5 years for standards and 13.7 to 14.2 for miniatures, so the gap is not just noise.

The study sorted breeds by size and put miniature Dachshunds with the small dogs and standards with the medium ones. Small purebreds as a group had a median of 12.7 years and medium purebreds 12.5, so both kinds of Dachshund outlived their size group by more than half a year.

Only 11 of the 155 breeds had a longer median than the miniature Dachshund. The study's hazard ratios tell the same story: at any given age, a miniature Dachshund's risk of dying was about a third lower than a crossbreed's (0.65), and a standard Dachshund's about a quarter lower (0.77).

Does weight change how long a dachshund lives?

A lot. A 2019 study of neutered pet dogs at about 900 North American vet hospitals compared dogs that were overweight in middle age with dogs of normal body condition, in 12 breeds. It only included dogs that were between 6.5 and 8.5 years old and still under care at 9.5, so its medians run higher than figures that count every dog from birth:

Dachshunds in the 2019 study Male Female
Normal body condition 16.4 years 16.4 years
Overweight 14.1 years 14.1 years

That is a gap of more than two years. Overweight Dachshunds had 2.77 times the risk of death of normal-weight ones, the second-highest hazard ratio of the 12 breeds after the Yorkshire Terrier. Your vet can tell you whether your Dachshund is carrying extra weight, and how to change that.

How common are back problems in dachshunds?

Common. The authors of a 2026 study call disc disease of the spine (intervertebral disc disease, or IVDD) a substantial health problem in Dachshunds. Their study, in the Journal of Veterinary Internal Medicine, looked at spinal health in 4,295 Dachshunds from the UK, Norway and Denmark. In the UK part, built from the 2021 DachsLife owner survey, 310 of 1,134 Dachshunds aged 10 or older (27%) had owner-reported signs of disc disease. The share varied a lot by type:

In all three countries, smooth-haired Dachshunds had worse spinal health measures than the other coat types. Size made a less consistent difference, though small smooth-haired dogs came out worst in 12 of 15 comparisons. The study measured spinal health, not lifespan, so it cannot say how many years back trouble takes off. Any change in how your Dachshund walks, climbs or holds its back is worth a call to your vet.

How long do dachshunds live, male vs female?

About the same, as far as we could find. In the 2019 study, male and female Dachshunds had identical medians, both at normal weight and when overweight. The 2024 UK study does not split each breed by sex; across all dogs, females lived a median 12.7 years and males 12.4.

How long can a dachshund live?

Past 20, but rarely. Guinness World Records verified a miniature Dachshund called Funny, who lived in Osaka, Japan, at 21 years 169 days on 12 November 2020, when Funny was the world's oldest living dog. That is far outside the usual range.

When is a dachshund considered a senior?

At about 9.9 for a standard and 10.5 for a miniature. The 2019 AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines say the senior stage starts in the last 25% of the estimated lifespan: 75% of 13.2 years is 9.9, and 75% of 14.0 is 10.5. Our senior dog age chart by breed does the same sum for 36 breeds.

VCA Animal Hospitals' weight guide gives 9 to 11 for dogs under 20 lb and 7 to 9 for dogs of 21 to 50 lb, so a heavy standard Dachshund might be called senior earlier by weight than by breed. For senior dogs VCA advises:

  1. Vet visits every six months instead of once a year.
  2. A check for arthritis pain and a talk about whether a senior diet would help.
  3. Regular exercise, stopping when your dog starts hanging back on walks.
  4. Soft places to sleep, and rugs or carpet on slippery floors.
  5. New toys and tricks to keep the mind busy.

Making the most of the years you have

A Dachshund that reaches 14 has years of the same small things: the same spot on the sofa, the same claws on the floor. Our senior dog bucket list has ideas sorted by how much energy a dog has.

Questions people ask

How long do dachshunds live on average?

About 13 to 14 years. A 2024 Dogs Trust study of 584,734 UK dogs found a median lifespan of 13.2 years for standard Dachshunds and 14.0 years for miniature Dachshunds, against 12.5 for all dogs. The AKC lists 12 to 16 years.

Do miniature dachshunds live longer than standard?

In the largest study, yes, by about 0.8 years: a median of 14.0 for miniatures (7,342 dogs) and 13.2 for standards (2,305 dogs), with confidence ranges that do not overlap. The study counted miniatures as small dogs and standards as medium, and both beat the averages for their size groups.

Do female dachshunds live longer than males?

Not in the one Dachshund figure by sex we found. In a 2019 North American study of neutered dogs that reached middle age, normal-weight male and female Dachshunds both had a median lifespan of 16.4 years. Across all breeds in a 2024 UK study, females lived a median 12.7 years and males 12.4.

What is the oldest dachshund ever?

Guinness World Records verified a miniature Dachshund called Funny, of Osaka, Japan, at 21 years 169 days on 12 November 2020, when Funny was the world's oldest living dog.

At what age is a dachshund a senior?

At about 9.9 for a standard Dachshund and 10.5 for a miniature, using the AAHA definition of senior as the last 25% of expected lifespan and the 2024 UK medians. VCA's weight guide gives 9 to 11 for dogs under 20 lb and 7 to 9 for 21 to 50 lb. Ask your vet where your own dog sits.
