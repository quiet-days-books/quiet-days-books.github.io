In short

  • UK studies put the German Shepherd's typical lifespan at about 10 to 11 years: a median of 10.3 in vet records from 2013, and 11.3 in a 2024 study of 19,908 dogs.
  • Females lived longer than males in the VetCompass study: a median of 11.1 years against 9.7.
  • The 2017 study's authors said German Shepherds are not particularly short-lived for a dog of their size.
  • Musculoskeletal problems and inability to stand were the most common recorded causes of death, ahead of cancer and spinal cord disorders.
  • By the AAHA 25% rule, a German Shepherd is a senior from about 8.5.

German Shepherds usually live about 10 to 11 years. UK vet records give a median of 10.3 years, with females living longer (11.1) than males (9.7). The largest study, of 19,908 German Shepherds, put the median at 11.3 years, a little below the 12.5 average for all dogs.

The figures differ for a few reasons. A median is the age by which half the dogs have died, while life expectancy at birth is an average that dogs dying young pull down. The 2024 study is much bigger and pooled data from insurers, breed registries, rehoming charities and vet groups, while the VetCompass studies use records from first-opinion vet practices.

The 2017 VetCompass median ranged from dogs that died very young to one that lived to 17. The middle half of the dogs died between 8.0 and 12.1 years.

How long do German Shepherds live, male vs female?

Females live longer. The 2017 VetCompass study found a median of 11.1 years for female German Shepherds and 9.7 years for males, a difference the authors found statistically significant.

So the sex gap in German Shepherds, about 1.4 years, was much wider in that study than the 0.3-year gap across all dogs. The study found no difference between males and females in how likely each of the 14 most common causes of death was.

Do German Shepherds have a short lifespan?

Shorter than average, but not short for their size. The 2024 study's median of 11.3 years for German Shepherds is below the 12.5 for all dogs and a little below the 11.9 for large breeds as a group.

The 2017 study compared its median of 10.3 years with 12.0 years for dogs of all breeds measured the same way. Its authors concluded that, despite health concerns about the breed, German Shepherds "are not particularly short-lived given their large body-size."

For how other breeds compare, see our page on how long dogs live, which also has the Golden Retriever as a large breed that lives longer than most.

What do German Shepherds usually die of?

The 2017 VetCompass study recorded a cause for 221 of its 272 deaths. Grouped broadly, the most common were:

Recorded cause of death Share of deaths with a cause
Musculoskeletal disorder 16.3%
Inability to stand 14.9%
Neoplasia (cancer) 14.5%
Spinal cord disorder 13.6%

The authors read this as a sign that problems with bones, joints and movement are a major part of the picture in this breed. They also point out that musculoskeletal disorders are often slow, progressive conditions that "do not necessarily lead to early death", though they can affect a dog's welfare for a long time.

That is general information about a population, not a guide to any one dog. If your German Shepherd is stiff, slow to get up or unsteady on its back legs, that is a reason to see your vet rather than to wait.

Does weight affect how long a German Shepherd lives?

It does, though less than in many breeds. A 2019 study of 50,787 neutered pet dogs at North American vet hospitals compared dogs that were overweight in middle age with dogs of normal body condition, across 12 breeds. Overweight dogs had a shorter median lifespan in every breed.

German Shepherds showed the smallest effect of the 12. The study only counted dogs alive at 6.5 to 8.5 years, so its medians run higher than UK figures counted from birth:

German Shepherds in the 2019 study Male Female
Normal body condition 12.5 years 13.1 years
Overweight 12.1 years 12.5 years

In the 2017 UK study, overweight or obesity was recorded in 5.18% of the German Shepherds whose records were checked. Your vet can score your dog's body condition and tell you whether to adjust its food.

When is a German Shepherd a senior?

At about 8.5, by the vets' definition. The American Animal Hospital Association treats a dog as senior in the last 25% of its expected lifespan, and 75% of the 2024 median of 11.3 years is about 8.5.

VCA Animal Hospitals' weight guide puts dogs of 51 to 100 lb, where most German Shepherds sit, at senior from about 6 to 7. Our senior dog age chart by breed explains the two methods and what changes once a dog crosses the line.

Making a German Shepherd's later years easier

VCA's advice for older dogs fits this breed well, given how often movement is the problem:

  1. See the vet every six months instead of once a year.
  2. Ask your vet to check for arthritis pain.
  3. Keep exercising regularly, and watch for signs your dog has had enough, like hanging back on walks.
  4. Put rugs or carpet down on slippery hard floors.
  5. Give your dog soft places to sleep.
  6. Keep its mind busy with new toys and tricks.

Questions people ask

How long do German Shepherds live on average?

About 10 to 11 years. A VetCompass study of UK vet records found a median of 10.3 years, a 2022 UK life-table study gave a life expectancy at birth of 10.16 years, and a 2024 Dogs Trust study of 19,908 German Shepherds found a median of 11.3 years.

How long do male German Shepherds live?

A median of 9.7 years in the 2017 VetCompass study of German Shepherds in UK vet practices, against 11.1 years for females. In a 2019 North American study of dogs that reached middle age, normal-weight males had a median of 12.5 years, and overweight males 12.1.

How long do female German Shepherds live?

A median of 11.1 years in the 2017 VetCompass study of UK German Shepherds, about a year and a half longer than males at 9.7. In a 2019 North American study of dogs alive in middle age, normal-weight females had a median lifespan of 13.1 years and overweight females 12.5.

What do German Shepherds usually die of?

In the 2017 VetCompass study, the most common recorded causes of death were musculoskeletal disorders (16.3%), inability to stand (14.9%), cancer (14.5%) and spinal cord disorders (13.6%). The authors note that musculoskeletal problems often develop slowly and do not necessarily cause early death.

When is a German Shepherd considered a senior?

At about 8.5, using the AAHA definition of senior as the last 25% of expected lifespan and the 2024 UK median of 11.3 years. VCA's weight guide puts dogs of 51 to 100 lb at about 6 to 7. Ask your vet where your own dog sits.
