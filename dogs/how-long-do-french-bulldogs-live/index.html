In short

  • The best current figure is a median of 9.8 years, from 11,821 French Bulldogs in a 2024 study of UK dogs.
  • Only 8 of the 155 breeds in that study had a shorter median, and all but one of those are large breeds.
  • Older vet-record studies gave 3.6 and 4.5 years because most UK Frenchies were still young when they were counted.
  • In the UK life tables, 13.1% of French Bulldogs died in their first year, against 1.7% of dogs overall.
  • By the AAHA 25% rule, a French Bulldog is a senior from about 7.4.

French Bulldogs live about 10 years. The largest study so far found a median lifespan of 9.8 years among 11,821 French Bulldogs in the UK, one of the lowest figures of any breed. Older vet-record studies put it at 3.6 and 4.5 years, but they counted a population of mostly young dogs, which drags the number down.

How long do French Bulldogs live on average?

Three UK studies have measured it, and their results look like they describe different breeds:

The American Kennel Club lists 10 to 12 years, which is close to the 2024 figure.

The 2024 study is the one to lean on. It is many times larger, it pooled records from 18 sources (insurers, vet groups, breed registries and rehoming charities), and it uses a survival method that also counts the dogs still alive. The two VetCompass studies only see the deaths that happened in a short window, and for French Bulldogs that window caught a very young population.

Why do some studies say French Bulldogs live only 4 years?

Because in the UK the breed boomed, and young populations produce young deaths. The 2018 study found that French Bulldogs made up 0.02% of puppies born into its practices in 2003 and 1.46% in 2013. The median age of all the Frenchies under vet care in 2013 was 1.3 years. The authors wrote that the profile of a stable population was likely to be quite different, with substantially greater longevity.

The 2022 life-table paper makes the same point with Kennel Club numbers: registrations of French Bulldogs rose from 2,771 in 2011 to 39,266 in 2020. Its authors said this tilts their data towards younger dogs and so towards early deaths, and that their French Bulldog sample was small.

So 3.6 and 4.53 describe what happened in those years. They do not forecast the life of a Frenchie puppy today.

Are French Bulldogs at risk when young?

Yes, and a young population does not explain all of it. In the 2022 life tables, 13.1% of French Bulldogs died between birth and their first birthday and 13.6% between one and two, against 1.7% and 1.6% for dogs overall. The authors listed French Bulldogs among six breeds with a much higher chance of dying before adulthood.

They also noted that the Frenchie's risk of death was rather uniform across all ages. Their remaining life expectancy fell below 1.5 years at age 10 to 11.

The 2018 study lists the breed's reported predispositions: breathing problems from the short face (brachycephalic obstructive airway syndrome), difficult births, corneal ulcers, slipping kneecaps and several spinal diseases. In 2013 the most common problems actually recorded by vets were ear inflammation (14.0%), diarrhoea (7.5%) and conjunctivitis (3.2%), and the airway syndrome was recorded in 2.4%, more often in males than females.

Do French Bulldogs live shorter lives than other small dogs?

Yes, by a wide margin. In the 2024 study, small purebreds as a group had a median of 12.7 years. Flat-faced (brachycephalic) purebreds had 11.2. French Bulldogs had 9.8, tied with the Bulldog.

Only 8 of the 155 breeds had a shorter median, and seven of them are classed as large breeds in the study, among them the Mastiff and St Bernard; the eighth is the Affenpinscher. The study's hazard ratio for French Bulldogs was 2.22: at any given age their risk of dying was more than twice a crossbreed's, and only four breeds scored higher. Our page on how long Pugs live covers another flat-faced breed, and how long dogs live gives the picture across breeds and sizes.

What do French Bulldogs usually die of?

The only breakdown we found is from the 2018 study, and it reflects that young 2013 population. Of 84 deaths with a recorded cause:

  • brain disorders, 11.9%, at a median age of 2.1 years
  • spinal cord disorders, 9.5%, at 4.0 years
  • upper airway disorders, 7.1%, at 2.5 years
  • lower airway disorders, 7.1%, at 0.9 years
  • mass lesions, 7.1%, at 7.0 years

The authors expected the causes to shift towards diseases of ageing as the population grows older. If you worry about your own Frenchie's breathing, ask your vet.

How long do French Bulldogs live, male vs female?

In the 2018 study, females that died had a median age of 4.0 years and males 3.5, but the authors found the difference was not significant. With 38 females and 54 males, that is not surprising. Across all dogs in the 2024 study, females lived a median 12.7 years and males 12.4.

How long can a French Bulldog live?

We found no verified record to quote. The oldest French Bulldog in the 2018 study was 12.2. The 2022 life-table paper cites a Japanese figure of 10.2 years for French Bulldogs against the UK's 4.53, and its authors note that the Japanese tables came from a smaller sample.

When is a French Bulldog a senior?

At about 7.4. The 2019 AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines define the senior stage as the last 25% of the estimated lifespan, and 75% of 9.8 years is 7.35. Our senior dog age chart by breed uses the same rule and explains why short-lived small breeds break weight charts.

VCA Animal Hospitals' weight guide would put a dog under 20 lb at senior from about 9 to 11, so a Frenchie reaches the line earlier than its size suggests. From then, VCA advises vet visits every six months instead of once a year, a check for arthritis pain, a talk about diet, regular but gentler exercise, soft places to sleep and rugs on slippery floors.

Making the most of the years you have

A Frenchie's life is shorter than most dogs'. The days in it are mostly ordinary ones, on the sofa and on short walks. Questions people ask

How long do French Bulldogs live on average?

About 10 years. A 2024 Dogs Trust study of 584,734 UK dogs included 11,821 French Bulldogs and found a median lifespan of 9.8 years. The AKC lists 10 to 12 years. Two UK vet-record studies gave much lower figures, 3.6 and 4.53 years, because the breed's population there was very young.

Why do some studies say French Bulldogs live only 4 years?

Because they counted the deaths of a population that was mostly puppies and young adults. UK French Bulldogs had a median age of 1.3 years in 2013, and Kennel Club registrations rose from 2,771 in 2011 to 39,266 in 2020. With few old Frenchies around yet, most recorded deaths were early ones.

Do French Bulldogs live shorter lives than other small dogs?

Yes. In the 2024 UK study, small purebreds had a median lifespan of 12.7 years and flat-faced purebreds 11.2, while French Bulldogs had 9.8. At any given age, their risk of dying was 2.22 times that of crossbreeds, one of the five highest hazard ratios of 155 breeds.

Do female French Bulldogs live longer than males?

There was no clear difference in the one Frenchie study that compared them: in 2013 UK vet records, females that died had a median age of 4.0 years and males 3.5, a gap the authors found was not significant. Across all breeds in a 2024 UK study, females lived a median 12.7 years and males 12.4.

At what age is a French Bulldog a senior?

At about 7.4 years, using the AAHA definition of senior as the last 25% of expected lifespan and the 2024 UK median of 9.8 years. That is earlier than a weight chart suggests for a small dog. Ask your vet where your own Frenchie sits.
