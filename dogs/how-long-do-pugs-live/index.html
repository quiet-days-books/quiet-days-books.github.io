In short

  • In the largest study so far, about 10,000 UK Pugs had a median lifespan of 11.6 years, below the 12.7 median for small purebred dogs.
  • Australian vet records for 691 Pugs that died give a median age at death of 10 years; a Tokyo pet-cemetery study gives 12.6.
  • A UK life table puts a newborn Pug's life expectancy at 7.65 years, but it counted only 197 Pugs, and many Pugs die young.
  • Breathing problems from the flat face, seizures and spinal disease were the leading causes of death in Australia.
  • By the AAHA 25% rule, a Pug is a senior from about 8.7.

Pugs usually live about 10 to 12 years. The largest study so far, of about 10,000 UK Pugs, found a median lifespan of 11.6 years, so half lived longer than that. Australian vet records of Pugs that had died give a median age at death of 10, and a Japanese study 12.6. Breed sites often say 12 to 15, which is more than any of the measurements below.

How long do pugs live on average?

Four studies have counted real Pugs, in three countries. Their figures run from 7.65 to 12.6 years:

The numbers are not measuring quite the same thing. A median is the age by which half the dogs have died. The Australian median is the middle age at death of the 691 Pugs that died during the study, and the authors point out that many younger Pugs were still alive and left out of it, so it likely sits below the true figure. Life expectancy at birth is an average, and an average is pulled down hard by the dogs that die very young. The 2024 UK study is also far bigger than the others and pooled records from 18 sources, including breed registries, insurers, rehoming charities and vet groups. The Japanese figure comes from pet cemeteries, so it only includes Pugs whose owners buried or cremated them there.

Why does one study say pugs live only 7.65 years?

The 7.65 figure is real, and it is quoted on many sites, but it needs reading carefully. It comes from the 2022 UK life table, which followed dogs that died in first-opinion vet practices from 2016 to 2020. Three things push it down.

The sample was small. The Pug table rested on 197 dogs, and the authors listed the Pug among the breeds whose small numbers made their tables less certain. Pugs in that study also had a much higher chance of dying before adulthood than dogs overall. And the UK Pug population was very young. Pugs went from under 1% of dogs born in England before 2008 to 2.8% of those born in 2013, and a 2022 VetCompass study found that the median age of Pugs under vet care was 2.36 years, against 4.44 for other dogs.

The same paper set its own figure beside Japan's: 7.65 years for UK Pugs and 12.8 in Japan. The authors noted that the Japanese tables came from a smaller sample, which may explain part of the gap.

Do pugs live long for a small dog?

No. Small dogs usually outlive big ones, and in the 2024 UK study the small purebreds as a group had a median of 12.7 years. Pugs came in more than a year below that, at 11.6.

The study also gives a hazard ratio against crossbreeds. For Pugs it was 1.34: at any given age, a Pug's risk of dying was about a third higher than a crossbreed's. The flat face is the usual explanation. Our page on how long French Bulldogs live covers another flat-faced breed, and how long dogs live has the picture across all breeds and sizes.

When do pugs usually die?

In two waves, in the Australian data. The study of 691 Pugs that died found that age at death had two peaks: one under a year old and one between 10 and 12. The largest single group of deceased Pugs, 35%, were over 12 years old.

Among Pugs that died under one year old, the top cause was traumatic injury. The later peak is the one most owners will meet, and the oldest Pugs in the study reached 17 and 18.

This is why a single number says little about your own dog. A Pug that is already five or six has come through the first risky stretch.

What do pugs usually die of?

In the Australian study, the most common single causes of death were:

  • brachycephalic obstructive airway syndrome (BOAS), the breathing problem caused by the flat face, 8.2%
  • seizures, 6.7%
  • degenerative spinal cord disorder, 4.7%

Neurological causes, taken together, were the most common reason owners and vets chose euthanasia (29.6%). Among Pugs that died without euthanasia, respiratory causes came first (25%), and deaths from breathing problems were found at every age. The authors link both groups to the flat-faced head shape.

The UK figures point the same way. In 2022, Pugs had 53.9 times the odds of having BOAS recorded by a vet compared with other dogs, and 1.86 times the odds of having at least one disorder recorded in a year. A Pug that snores loudly, struggles in the heat or tires fast on walks is worth a conversation with your vet, rather than a guess from a chart.

Do female pugs live longer than males?

Not by the median, in the one study that split it. In Australia, both sexes had a median age at death of 10 years, but males had a significantly lower chance of survival than females, mostly between the ages of 4 and 10. The spread was similar: the middle half of females died between 6 and 12, and of males between 7 and 13.

The same study reports that entire Pugs died earlier than neutered ones. The authors did not have the age at neutering, so this shows a link, not a cause, and we do not quote their figures for it. Whether and when to neuter your Pug is a question for your vet.

Does weight change how long a pug lives?

Weight is the most common problem recorded in Pugs. In a 2016 VetCompass study of Pugs in England, overweight or obesity was the problem vets recorded most often in a year, in 13.18% of the dogs. Corneal disorders (8.72%) and ear inflammation (7.53%) came next.

None of the Pug studies measured lifespan by body condition, so we cannot give a Pug-specific number. In other breeds, overweight dogs had shorter median lifespans; our page on how long Labs live has those figures. For a breed that already breathes through a narrow airway, extra weight is not a small thing. Ask your vet whether your Pug is at a good weight.

When is a pug considered a senior?

At about 8.7, by the vets' definition. The 2019 AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines say the senior stage starts in the last 25% of the estimated lifespan, and 75% of the UK median of 11.6 years is 8.7. Our senior dog age chart by breed does the same sum for 36 breeds.

That puts a Pug into its senior years earlier than its size suggests. The UK and Australian studies suggest what to keep an eye on as the years go by:

  1. Breathing: noisy breathing, struggling in warm weather or tiring fast on walks.
  2. Weight: overweight was the problem recorded most often in English Pugs.
  3. Eyes: corneal problems came second.
  4. Walking and balance: spinal cord disease was among the top causes of death in Australia.

Any change in these is worth raising at your next vet visit, or sooner if it comes on quickly.

Making the most of the years you have

Eleven or twelve years with a Pug is mostly sofa, short walks and snoring. Our senior dog bucket list sorts ideas by how much energy a dog has left, which suits a breed that tires early.

One activity in our book One Day, You'll Miss This is called "Record Their Breathing While They Sleep". For most dogs it is a quiet sound you stop noticing. For a Pug it is the loudest sound in the house, and the book asks you to record two minutes of it to play back later with your eyes closed. Another, "Give Them Their Perfect Day", asks you to build a whole day out of things your dog already loves, at their energy rather than yours.

Questions people ask

How long do pugs live on average?

About 10 to 12 years. A 2024 Dogs Trust study of about 10,000 UK Pugs found a median lifespan of 11.6 years. Australian vet records of Pugs that had died gave a median age at death of 10 years, and a study of Tokyo pet cemeteries 12.6. Breed sites often say 12 to 15, which is higher than any of these measurements.

Why do some sources say pugs only live 7 years?

That figure comes from a 2022 UK life table, which put a Pug's life expectancy at birth at 7.65 years. It is an average that is pulled down by Pugs that die young, it counted only 197 Pugs, and the authors noted that small breed samples give less certain tables. A Pug that reaches adulthood can expect more than that.

Do female pugs live longer than males?

In an Australian study of 691 Pugs that died, both sexes had a median age at death of 10 years, but males had a significantly lower chance of survival than females, mostly between the ages of 4 and 10.

What do pugs usually die of?

In the Australian VetCompass study, the most common single causes of death were brachycephalic obstructive airway syndrome (8.2%), seizures (6.7%) and degenerative spinal cord disorder (4.7%). Neurological causes were the most common reason for euthanasia, and respiratory problems the most common cause of unassisted death.

At what age is a pug a senior?

At about 8.7 years, using the AAHA definition of senior as the last 25% of expected lifespan and the UK median of 11.6. Your vet can tell you where your own Pug sits.
