In short

  • In the largest UK study, of 584,734 dogs, the median Chihuahua lifespan was 11.8 years, a little below crossbreeds at 12.0.
  • UK vet-record studies give lower figures, 7.9 and 8.2 years, because many Chihuahuas die young and the breed's population is young.
  • Chihuahuas that get past the risky early years often live long: in UK life tables they were the last of 18 breeds to run short of expected years.
  • Guinness verified a Chihuahua called TobyKeith at 21 years and 66 days in 2022.
  • Dental disease and extra weight were the most common recorded problems. Ask your vet about teeth and weight at every check-up.

Chihuahuas live a median of 11.8 years, according to the largest lifespan study so far: 584,734 UK dogs, published in Scientific Reports in 2024. Lifespans in the breed spread widely. Many Chihuahuas die young, which pulls some averages down to about 8 years, while those that get through the early years have a good chance of a long life, and a few pass 20.

How long do Chihuahuas live on average?

The best single figure is 11.8 years. That is the median age at death of 3,848 Chihuahuas in the study by the charity Dogs Trust, with a 95% confidence range of 11.5 to 11.9. Half of those dogs died younger than that, half older.

Two other UK studies, both built from vet clinic records by the Royal Veterinary College's VetCompass programme, give lower numbers. Here are all three side by side:

Study Chihuahuas who had died Figure for Chihuahuas
McMillan et al. 2024, Dogs Trust, 584,734 UK dogs 3,848 Median lifespan 11.8 years
Teng et al. 2022, VetCompass life tables, deaths 2016 to 2020 453 Life expectancy at birth 7.91 years
O'Neill et al. 2020, VetCompass, Chihuahuas seen by vets in 2016 102 Median age at death 8.2 years

The numbers measure slightly different things, and the gap between them is the interesting part.

Why do some figures say Chihuahuas live only 8 years?

Because many Chihuahuas die young, and in 2016 most UK Chihuahuas were young. The O'Neill study found the breed's share of puppies born in the UK rose from 1.01% in 2005 to 5.35% in 2016, and the median age of the Chihuahuas under vet care was only 2.8 years. With few old Chihuahuas around yet, the deaths in that year were mostly early ones. The authors say this would bias the age at death results downwards.

The life tables tell the same story from another angle. In the Teng study, Chihuahuas had a higher chance of dying than dogs overall before about age 13, and a much higher chance before age 4. Of the 18 breeds analysed, Chihuahuas were the last to see their remaining life expectancy fall below 1.5 years, at age 15 to 16. The authors read this as a high variation of lifespans in the breed.

So both things are true. A Chihuahua puppy faces more early risk than an average dog. A Chihuahua that reaches middle age has a good chance of a long life.

Do Chihuahuas live long compared with other dogs?

About average, and shorter than many small breeds. In the 2024 study, all 584,734 dogs together had a median lifespan of 12.5 years, small purebreds 12.7 and crossbreeds 12.0. The Chihuahua, classed there as small and flat-faced, came in at 11.8, and its risk of dying at any given age was 1.17 times that of crossbreeds.

How long can Chihuahuas live for?

Some reach their late teens and a few pass 20. The oldest Chihuahua in the O'Neill study was 17. Guinness World Records verified a Chihuahua called TobyKeith, of Greenacres, Florida, at 21 years and 66 days on 16 March 2022, when he was confirmed as the world's oldest living dog. The UK Kennel Club, as quoted in the O'Neill study, gives the Chihuahua lifespan as over 12 years.

Do female Chihuahuas live longer than males?

In the O'Neill study, yes, by a wide margin: females that died had a median age of 10.2 years, males 6.9. The authors caution that these results are likely to be weighted towards early-life deaths in a young population. Across all breeds in the 2024 study the difference was small: females 12.7 years, males 12.4.

How long do Chihuahuas live in human years?

There is no honest one-line conversion for a single breed, and the familiar rule of seven human years per dog year is a rough shortcut, not a measurement. Our page on dog years to human years explains the methods researchers use and what each one assumes. In plain dog years, the figure to keep in mind for a Chihuahua is the 11.8-year median, with plenty of dogs going well beyond it.

For the wider picture across breeds and sizes, see how long dogs live.

When is a Chihuahua a senior?

At about 8.9 years. The 2019 AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines say the senior stage starts at the last 25% of the estimated lifespan, and 75% of 11.8 is 8.85. Our senior dog age chart by breed applies the same rule to 36 breeds.

That puts the Chihuahua's senior line a little earlier than a simple chart by weight would suggest. It is a guide, not a date: your vet can tell you more from your own dog than any median can.

What helps a Chihuahua live a long life?

Nothing guarantees years, but the O'Neill study shows where Chihuahuas most often need care. Among 5,660 Chihuahuas with full records, the most common problems were:

  • periodontal (gum) disease, in 13.5%
  • obesity, in 5.9%
  • retained baby teeth, in 5.7%
  • anal sac impaction, in 4.9%
  • aggression, in 4.2%

Dental problems of all kinds affected 19.0%, and the authors concluded the breed should be considered predisposed to dental disease. They also expect gum disease and obesity to become more common as the young Chihuahua population ages. Where a cause of death was recorded, heart disease was the most common.

What you can do with that:

  1. Ask your vet to check teeth and gums at every visit, and ask what home tooth care suits your dog.
  2. Weigh your Chihuahua now and then. On a dog whose median adult weight in the study was 3.4 kg, a few hundred grams matters.
  3. Book check-ups every six months once your dog passes the senior line, as VCA Animal Hospitals advises for senior pets.
  4. Mention anything new to your vet rather than putting it down to age.

Questions people ask

How long do Chihuahuas live on average?

About 11.8 years, going by the median of 3,848 Chihuahuas that had died in a 2024 study of 584,734 UK dogs. UK vet-record studies give lower averages, near 8 years, because many Chihuahuas die young and the breed's population is young. Half of the dogs in that study lived past 11.8.

Can a Chihuahua live 20 years?

Some do, though it is rare. Guinness World Records verified a Chihuahua called TobyKeith, from Florida, at 21 years and 66 days on 16 March 2022. In UK life tables, Chihuahuas were the breed whose remaining life expectancy stayed above 1.5 years the longest, until age 15 to 16.

Do female Chihuahuas live longer than males?

In one UK vet-record study, yes: females that died had a median age of 10.2 years and males 6.9. The authors warn the gap is weighted towards early deaths in a young population. Across all breeds in a 2024 study, females lived a median 12.7 years and males 12.4.

How long do Chihuahuas live in human years?

There is no reliable one-line conversion for a single breed, and the familiar 'times seven' rule is a shortcut, not a measurement. Our page on dog years to human years explains the methods people use. In plain dog years, the median Chihuahua lifespan in a large UK study is 11.8.

At what age is a Chihuahua a senior?

At about 8.9 years. Vets call a dog senior in the last 25% of its expected lifespan, according to AAHA, and 75% of the Chihuahua's 11.8-year median is about 8.9. A dog's own health counts more than the average, so ask your vet where yours sits.
