How the dog age calculator works

It looks up your dog's age in the AKC chart "How old is my dog in human years?", in the column for its weight. The chart has one row for each birthday from 1 to 16. Between two birthdays we read it on a straight line: a medium dog of 7 years and 6 months sits halfway between 47 and 51, so about 49. That step is our arithmetic, not the AKC's.

Outside the chart we give no number. For a puppy under a year the calculator tells you where the chart starts, and past 16 it tells you where the chart stops.

The AKC also quotes the American Veterinary Medical Association's rule of thumb for a medium-sized dog. The first year is about 15 human years, the second adds about 9, and each year after that about 5. The chart follows the first two steps, then splits by size from age 6.

Why size changes the answer

Small, medium and large dogs match on the chart up to 5. After that a bigger dog ages faster in human terms: at 10 a small dog is about 56, a large dog 66 and a giant dog 79.

Size predicts lifespan only roughly, though. Some small breeds live short lives, and some big ones live long. Our senior dog age chart by breed works it out breed by breed, and our page on how long dogs live has measured lifespans.

The DNA formula, and why it is for Labradors

The second figure comes from a 2020 study in Cell Systems by Wang and colleagues. They compared chemical marks on DNA that build up with age, called methylation, in 104 dogs aged 0.1 to 16 years, mostly Labrador Retrievers, with the same marks in people. The curve they published is:

human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31

Here ln is the natural logarithm. A 4-year-old dog comes out at about 53, a 12-year-old at about 71.

The authors write that the study "focused exclusively on Labrador retrievers" and that breeds with very different lifespans could give different curves. So the calculator prints it as a second figure, for dogs of about Labrador size, from age 1. Below a year the curve falls steeply and there is no chart figure to set it against. Our page on dog years to human years puts the formula and the chart side by side and explains where they disagree.

How the life stage is worked out

The stage names come from the 2019 AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines, written for vets by a task force of the American Animal Hospital Association:

  1. Puppy: from birth until rapid growth stops, at about 6 to 9 months depending on breed and size.
  2. Young adult: until the dog is grown up in body and behaviour, which most dogs are by 3 to 4.
  3. Mature adult: until the last quarter of the expected lifespan.
  4. Senior: that last quarter.

AAHA prints no ages for the senior stage. It leaves the estimate to the vet, mostly from the breed's usual lifespan. So for the senior line the calculator uses the weight bands of VCA Animal Hospitals: about 9 to 11 for a small dog, 7 to 9 for a medium dog, 6 to 7 for a large dog and 4 to 5 for a giant. Inside that band it says "senior, or close to it".

The same guidelines explain why vets skip human years. The task force defines each stage by age and by what the dog is like, "rather than attempt to calculate age equivalents to humans".

What the number is good for

Mostly for seeing the stage of life. A 10-year-old large dog at about 66 is not middle-aged any more, even if it still meets you at the door. The number says nothing about your dog's health or how long it will live, so ask your vet about those. AAHA advises at least two vet exams a year for a senior dog.

Questions people ask

How does a dog age calculator work?

This one looks up your dog's age in the American Kennel Club chart, in the column for its adult weight, and reads between birthdays on a straight line. It adds the 2020 DNA formula built on Labradors and a life stage from the AAHA guidelines and VCA's senior bands. Every figure is an estimate.

Is there a dog age calculator by breed?

Not for human years that we could source. The AKC chart works by weight, and the DNA formula was built on Labradors only. Breed matters most for when a dog turns senior: our senior dog age chart by breed applies the AAHA rule to measured UK breed lifespans.

Is the dog years to human years calculator scientific?

In part. The DNA formula comes from a peer-reviewed study of 104 dogs, but of one breed. The AKC chart is a rule of thumb, and its page does not say how it was built. The vets' own AAHA guidelines skip human years altogether and define life stages instead.

How old is a 10 year old dog in human years?

About 56 for a small dog, 60 for a medium dog, 66 for a large dog and 79 for a giant dog, on the AKC chart. The Labrador DNA formula gives about 68.
