In short

  • Most dogs live 10 to 13 years. The largest study, of 584,734 UK dogs, found a median lifespan of 12.5 years.
  • Breed matters most: medians ran from 5.4 years for the Caucasian Shepherd Dog to 15.4 for the Lancashire Heeler.
  • Small and medium breeds lived longer than large ones (12.7 and 12.5 against 11.9 years), and flat-faced breeds shorter than others.
  • Females lived slightly longer than males, and dogs kept lean lived longer than overweight dogs in two separate studies.
  • Fewer than 1 in 20 dogs in the 2024 study were alive at 18.3 years.

Most dogs live 10 to 13 years. The largest study so far, of 584,734 dogs in the UK, found a median lifespan of 12.5 years, which means half the dogs lived longer than that. Breed makes the biggest difference: medians in that study ran from 5.4 years for the Caucasian Shepherd Dog to 15.4 for the Lancashire Heeler.

How long do dogs live on average?

About 12 years, give or take a year depending on which study you read. The three largest UK data sets give these figures:

Study Dogs Average lifespan
McMillan and colleagues, Dogs Trust, Scientific Reports 2024 584,734 dogs from 18 data sources, 284,734 of them deceased Median 12.5 years
O'Neill and colleagues, VetCompass, The Veterinary Journal 2013 5,095 deaths among 102,609 dogs at 86 practices in England Median 12.0 years
Teng and colleagues, VetCompass, Scientific Reports 2022 30,563 dogs that died from 2016 to 2020 Life expectancy at birth 11.23 years

The numbers differ partly because they measure different things. A median is the age by which half the dogs have died. Life expectancy at birth is an average, and it is pulled down by the dogs that die young. The dogs in each study also came from different places: the 2024 study pooled insurers, breed registries, rehoming charities and vet groups, while VetCompass uses records from first-opinion vet practices.

The 2024 paper sums up earlier work too: depending on the country and the breeds studied, average lifespans for dogs have been reported between 10.0 and 13.7 years.

Do small dogs live longer than big dogs?

Yes, on average. In the 2024 study small purebreds had a median lifespan of 12.7 years, medium 12.5 and large 11.9. The 2013 VetCompass study found the same direction: the heavier the dog, the shorter its life tended to be.

Face shape mattered as well. Breeds with medium-length faces had a median of 12.8 years, long-faced breeds 12.1 and flat-faced (brachycephalic) breeds 11.2. Medium-sized flat-faced breeds had a median of just 9.4 years.

That is why some small dogs break the size rule. The French Bulldog is small, but its median of 9.8 years is among the lowest in the study. The Golden Retriever is large and lives longer than the average small dog.

Do mixed breed dogs live longer?

The big studies disagree, and it is worth knowing why. The 2013 VetCompass study of dogs in England found that crossbreeds lived 1.2 years longer than purebreds. The 2024 Dogs Trust study found the reverse: a median of 12.7 years for purebreds and 12.0 for crossbreeds.

The 2024 authors point to who is counted as a crossbreed. Their crossbred group included designer crosses such as the Labradoodle along with dogs of unknown mixed ancestry, and they say those groups should be studied separately. Their data also came partly from insurers and breed registries, which the authors note tend to over-represent purebred dogs.

So a mixed breed dog of medium size is likely to live around 12 years, but neither study can promise a crossbreed a longer life than a purebred.

Do female dogs live longer than males?

Slightly. In the 2024 study the median was 12.7 years for females and 12.4 for males, and the gap held for both purebreds and crossbreeds. The 2022 VetCompass life tables found the same: 11.41 years for females and 11.07 for males at birth.

In some breeds the gap is bigger. A VetCompass study of German Shepherds found a median of 11.1 years for females and 9.7 for males.

Does neutering or weight change how long a dog lives?

Both have been linked with lifespan, with caveats.

In the 2022 VetCompass life tables, neutered females had a life expectancy of 11.98 years and neutered males 11.49, against 10.50 and 10.58 for entire dogs. The authors warn that these figures "should be interpreted with great caution": a dog has to live long enough to be neutered before it can be counted as neutered, which flatters the neutered group. Whether and when to neuter is a decision to make with your vet.

The weight evidence is clearer:

  1. In a lifelong study of 48 Labrador Retrievers (Kealy and colleagues, 2002), one dog in each pair was fed 25% less than its pair-mate from 8 weeks old. The leaner dogs had a significantly longer median lifespan, and signs of chronic disease generally appeared later.
  2. A 2019 study of 50,787 neutered pet dogs at about 900 North American vet hospitals compared dogs that were overweight in middle age with dogs of normal body condition. In all 12 breeds studied, the overweight dogs had a shorter median lifespan, by 5 months in male German Shepherds and up to 2 years 6 months in male Yorkshire Terriers.

If you are unsure whether your dog is at a healthy weight, your vet can score its body condition and suggest how much to feed.

What is the longest a dog can live?

Few dogs pass 18. In the 2024 study, 95% of both purebred and crossbred dogs had died by about 18.3 years.

The best-known record is Bluey, an Australian cattle dog owned by Les Hall of Rochester, Victoria, who according to Guinness World Records lived from 1910 to 1939 and reached 29 years 5 months. In 2023 Guinness gave the title to Bobi, a dog from Portugal. After a review in February 2024 it said it "no longer has the evidence it needs to support Bobi's claim" and that it was not yet able to confirm a new holder.

Questions people ask

How long do dogs live on average?

About 12 years. The largest study so far, of 584,734 dogs in the UK published in 2024, found a median lifespan of 12.5 years. A UK study of dogs that died between 2016 and 2020 gave a life expectancy at birth of 11.23 years. Studies of different groups of dogs give figures between 10 and 13.7.

Which dog breeds live the longest?

In the 2024 Dogs Trust study, the breeds with the longest median lifespans were the Lancashire Heeler (15.4 years), Tibetan Spaniel (15.2), Shiba Inu (14.6) and Papillon (14.5). The Border Terrier, Lakeland Terrier and Schipperke followed at 14.2. Most of the longest-lived breeds were small.

Do small dogs live longer than big dogs?

On average, yes. In the 2024 UK study small purebreds had a median lifespan of 12.7 years, medium 12.5 and large 11.9. A 2013 study of dogs in England also found that higher bodyweight went with a shorter life. Some small breeds, such as the French Bulldog, are exceptions.

Do mixed breed dogs live longer?

The studies disagree. A 2013 VetCompass study of dogs in England found crossbreeds lived 1.2 years longer than purebreds. The 2024 Dogs Trust study found the opposite: a median of 12.7 years for purebreds and 12.0 for crossbreeds, including designer crosses. The authors say crossbreeds are too varied to treat as one group.

What is the longest a dog has lived?

Guinness World Records named Bluey, an Australian cattle dog who lived to 29 years 5 months (1910 to 1939), as the holder before Bobi. In February 2024 Guinness withdrew the title from Bobi and said it could not yet confirm a new holder.
